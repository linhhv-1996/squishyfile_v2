Free Video Compressor — No Uploads, No Limits

Why compress video with SquishyFile?

Most online video compressors make you upload your file to a server, wait in a queue, then download the result — slow, and a little unsettling if the footage is private. SquishyFile does everything right in your browser using WebAssembly: your video never leaves your device, and it keeps working even if your connection is spotty.

It's completely free, with no file-size cap and no watermark slapped on your export. Shrinking a 2GB family video down to a few hundred MB to send over chat, or prepping a clip so your website loads faster — SquishyFile handles both, as many times as you need.

Compress a video in 3 steps

1.Choose your video

Drag a file into the box above, or click to browse. MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI and most common formats are supported.

2.Set compression level or target size

Slide toward "Max squish" for the smallest file, or type an exact target size in MB if you need to hit a specific limit.

3.Hit Squish and download

The whole process runs on your device. When it's done, download the compressed MP4 directly — no server round-trip involved.

Frequently asked questions

What video formats can I upload?

MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM and most other common formats. The compressed output is always exported as MP4 for the widest compatibility.

Is there a limit on file size or how many videos I can compress?

No limits either way. Compress as many videos as you want, at any size — it's free, with no watermark added to your files.

What does "target size" do?

Enter the file size you need — say, under 50MB for an email attachment — and SquishyFile calculates the compression needed to hit it.

Is my video stored on your server?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser — not a single byte of your video is ever sent anywhere.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. SquishyFile runs in mobile browsers too, though larger videos may take longer to process depending on your device.
